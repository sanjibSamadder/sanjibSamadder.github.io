About me

I work with real-world, imperfect data: cleaning it, structuring it and turning it into something a business can act on. Over nine years I've done that in electronics retail, FMCG distribution, LPG supply, US real estate finance and, most recently, research and innovation consulting.

I'm now studying for an MSc in Data Science (with Industry Placement) at the University of Leicester and looking for a Data Scientist or ML placement starting in 2027.
